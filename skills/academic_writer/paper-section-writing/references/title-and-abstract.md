# Title and Abstract

Use this file for title and abstract drafting.

## Title job

The title should let a field reader infer:

- what problem or object the paper studies
- what action or mechanism is central
- why the paper is not just a generic study

## Weak title patterns

Avoid:

- `A Study of ...`
- `Towards ...`
- `An Approach to ...`
- vague nouns without a mechanism
- long strings of jargon

## Title templates

```text
Improving [task] with [mechanism]
Understanding [phenomenon] through [method]
Identifying [failure mode] in [system / setting]
[Mechanism] for [task] under [hard condition]
```

## Abstract job

The abstract is the compressed paper. It must include:

1. problem
2. gap
3. action
4. key result
5. implication

## Abstract template

```text
[Problem] is important in [field / application] because ...
However, existing work remains limited by ...
To address this issue, we [propose / introduce / show] ...
Across [datasets / benchmarks / settings], we find that ...
These results suggest that ...
```

## Strong abstract moves

- Start with the concrete problem, not a broad trend.
- Name the gap before naming the method.
- State the strongest result in measurable or specific terms.
- End with implication, not marketing.

## Blockers

The abstract is not ready if:

- it omits the gap
- it says "we propose" but not what the proposal changes
- it says "outperforms" without saying where or why
- it makes a claim not supported by the Results section
