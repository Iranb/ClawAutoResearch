# Thesis Crystallization

Use this before freezing `PAPER_PLAN.md`, especially when the experiments are real but the story still feels vague.

## Core Questions

Answer these in one sentence each:

- What is this paper arguing?
- What should a skeptical reviewer disagree with first?
- After reading the paper, what should the reader think differently?
- What did the field know before this paper?
- What do we know after this paper?

If you cannot answer all five crisply, the outline is not ready.

## Chapter "So What" Prompts

- Introduction: Why should the reader care right now?
- Related work: What gap or tension does the literature leave unresolved?
- Method: Why is this mechanism necessary, not just interesting?
- Results: What is the one finding the reader must remember?
- Discussion: What does the result change for the field?
- Conclusion: What is the final delta in one paragraph?

## Output Contract

Before leaving planning, make these explicit:

- `thesis_statement`
- `skeptic_response`
- `delta_statement`
- `chapter_purpose_map`

Each chapter purpose should point back to the same thesis. If a chapter cannot answer "so what?", narrow or delete it.
