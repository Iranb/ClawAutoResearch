# Benchmark Landscape (Survey)

## Goal

Explain what benchmark comparisons really mean.

## Must Include

1. Main datasets / benchmark clusters.
2. Primary metrics and where they differ.
3. Which comparisons are fair and which are shaky.
4. Backbone / modality / training-protocol caveats.
5. What benchmark coverage is still missing.

## Warning

Do not collapse incompatible settings into a single ranking table without warning the reader.
