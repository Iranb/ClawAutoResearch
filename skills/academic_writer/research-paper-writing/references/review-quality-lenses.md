# Review Quality Lenses

Review the paper through three lenses at the same time.

## Lens 1: Internal Validity

- What is the central claim?
- What evidence is supposed to support it?
- Is the warrant explicit?
- Would removing one piece of evidence collapse the argument?

If the answer to the last question is yes, flag the story as brittle.

## Lens 2: External Validity

- What population, task, or setting is the claim really about?
- Are the boundary conditions explicit?
- Is the paper overstating how far the finding generalizes?

Most manuscripts overclaim scope before they overclaim numbers.

## Lens 3: Contribution

- What was known before this paper?
- What is newly known after this paper?
- Is the delta meaningful, not just nonzero?
- Who benefits from this result?

If the contribution delta cannot be stated in one sentence, the paper is not ready to claim novelty clearly.

## Reviewer Trap Avoidance

- Do not confuse a long issue list with a strong review.
- State the single biggest risk first.
- Separate "unclear but fixable" from "unsupported and unsafe".
