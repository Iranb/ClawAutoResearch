# Discussion, Limitations, and Conclusion Review

## Review job

Check whether the ending interprets evidence honestly and closes within scope.

## Discussion checks

Revise if:

- interpretation is vague
- field-level implication is not tied to evidence
- important caveats are delayed or hidden

## Limitations checks

Revise if:

- scope boundary is missing
- failure cases are ignored
- deployment or generalization claims lack boundary

## Conclusion checks

Block if:

- conclusion introduces a new unsupported claim
- conclusion broadens beyond Results
- conclusion contradicts limitations

Revise if:

- conclusion only repeats abstract
- ending is promotional rather than precise

## Output focus

State the safest defensible takeaway.
