# Taxonomy (Survey)

## Goal

Organize the field into stable families without pretending every paper fits perfectly.

## Must Include

1. Family names and their organizing principle.
2. The core mechanism or assumption of each family.
3. Representative papers for each family.
4. Boundary cases and overlaps between families.
5. At least one paragraph comparing families, not just listing them.

## Warning

Do not make taxonomy look authoritative if the packet is noisy. Say when categories are tentative or partially overlapping.
