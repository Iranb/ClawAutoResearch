# Open Problems (Survey)

## Goal

Convert gaps into evidence-backed open problems.

## Must Include

1. What is unresolved.
2. Why current methods or evidence are insufficient.
3. Which missing comparisons / benchmarks / assumptions create the gap.
4. Why the problem matters for the next phase of the field.

## Rule

Open problems should come from observed evidence gaps, contradiction zones, or coverage limits, not from generic future-work boilerplate.
