# Discussion, Limitations, and Conclusion

Use this file when drafting or revising Discussion, Limitations, or Conclusion.

## Discussion job

Discussion should interpret the evidence and explain what changed in the reader's understanding.

## Discussion template

```text
These results indicate that ...
More specifically, they suggest that ...
At the same time, our findings remain limited to ...
Therefore, the main contribution of this work is best understood as ...
```

## Limitations job

Limitations should protect the paper from overclaiming.

## Limitations template

```text
Our study is primarily evaluated under ...
We have not yet tested ...
Therefore, our conclusions should be interpreted as applying mainly to ...
```

## Conclusion job

Conclusion should return to the original problem and leave a defensible takeaway.

## Conclusion template

```text
We addressed ...
Our main finding is that ...
Taken together, these results suggest ...
```

## Hard rules

- Do not introduce new results in Conclusion.
- Do not repeat the Abstract mechanically.
- Do not broaden the scope at the end.
- Do not hide limitations in vague future work.

## Ready check

These sections are ready only if:

- the takeaway is evidence-backed
- the boundary is explicit
- the ending is precise rather than promotional
