# Figure-Centric Writing

## Primary Principle
The anchor figure is not decoration. It is the visual proof of the paper’s causal story.

## Workflow
1. Decide the primary anchor figure first.
2. Make the introduction point to that figure early.
3. Use the figure to explain why the method is necessary.
4. Let tables and secondary figures support specific claims under that anchor.

## Checklist
- Can the reader understand the paper’s main move from the anchor figure alone?
- Does the figure expose the novel module or decision point clearly?
- Does each headline claim map to a visible evidence object (figure or table)?
- If the figure is weak, fix the figure or narrow the claim before polishing text.
