# Evidence Synthesis (Survey)

## Goal

Turn paper-level notes into field-level understanding.

## Must Include

1. Shared comparison axes across representative methods.
2. Strongest wins and strongest weaknesses.
3. Tradeoffs instead of one-dimensional rankings.
4. Contradictions or non-comparable claims when they exist.
5. At least one paragraph that says what the field currently knows with confidence.

## Good Pattern

- opening synthesis claim
- family / paper comparison
- tradeoff paragraph
- disagreement / caveat paragraph
- takeaway paragraph
