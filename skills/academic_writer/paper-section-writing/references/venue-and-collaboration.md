# Venue and Collaboration

Use this file when adapting a draft for top conferences, journals, or multi-author writing.

## Conference writing

Top conference writing usually requires:

- tight page budget
- self-contained main text
- strong abstract / intro / figure 1
- no dependence on appendix for core proof
- fast claim-evidence visibility

Hard rule:

- If a claim is central to acceptance, its evidence must appear in the main text.

## Journal writing

Top journal writing usually requires:

- broader significance
- readable framing for adjacent-field readers
- transparent methods and reporting
- clear data/code/ethics/disclosure statements when relevant
- stronger editorial first impression

Hard rule:

- The opening must explain why the result matters beyond a narrow implementation detail.

## Shared writing protocol

For multi-author drafts:

- use a shared outline
- assign section owners
- review figures before prose expands
- keep terminology and notation stable
- sync Abstract, Introduction, Contributions, Results, and Conclusion after every major story change

Hard rule:

- Do not stitch independently written sections without a full consistency pass.
