# Writing Quality Check

These are writing-quality rules, not AI-evasion tricks.

## Flagged Patterns

- Overused abstract words: prefer precise technical wording over vague intensity words.
- Throat-clearing openers: do not waste sentences on "this section discusses..." style meta-commentary.
- Em dash and semicolon overuse: if punctuation becomes a rhythm crutch, simplify.
- Uniform paragraph length: vary paragraph length naturally; not every paragraph should look templated.
- Synonym cycling: repeat technical terms consistently instead of rotating vocabulary for style.
- Metronomic sentence rhythm: break sequences where every sentence has the same length and cadence.

## Quick Sweep

For each section ask:

- Is there any filler phrase that can be deleted without loss?
- Are the claims phrased more strongly than the evidence allows?
- Do paragraph lengths all look suspiciously similar?
- Are there repeated "safe AI words" where a sharper term would be better?
- Does the prose sound like polished thinking, or like evenly spaced summary text?

## Repair Strategy

- Replace vague emphasis with a concrete descriptor.
- Cut meta-sentences before rewriting content sentences.
- Keep stable terminology for key objects, claims, modules, and datasets.
- Use short sentences for emphasis and longer ones only when real complexity demands it.
