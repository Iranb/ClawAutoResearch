# Writing Judgment Framework

Use this when choosing what deserves heavy rewriting and what should stay short.

## Clarity Test

For every paragraph ask:

- If I remove this paragraph, does the paper still make sense?

Interpret the answer:

- Yes, and nothing important is lost: delete it.
- Yes, but context is lost: keep it short because it is supporting material.
- No, the argument breaks: this is a load-bearing paragraph and deserves the most careful writing.

## Reader's Journey

At every major section and paragraph boundary, the reader should be able to answer:

1. Where am I?
2. Why am I here?
3. What should I take away?
4. Where am I going next?

If any answer is unclear, rewrite transitions or section framing before polishing sentences.

## Section-Level "So What"

- Introduction: why this problem matters.
- Method: why this mechanism is necessary.
- Results: what changed empirically.
- Discussion: what the change means.
- Conclusion: what survives after scope narrowing.

## Practical Rule

Do not spend equal effort on every paragraph. Invest most in:

- the thesis paragraph
- the challenge paragraph
- the contribution paragraph
- the result summary paragraph
- the limitation/scope paragraph
