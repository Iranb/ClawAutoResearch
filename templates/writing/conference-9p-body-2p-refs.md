# Conference Writing Mode

Use this built-in mode for conference-style papers with:

- `9` pages main body
- `2` pages references
- `1-2` core ideas only
- reviewer-facing, concise technical storytelling

## Required Narrative Contract

- Thesis must fit in one sentence.
- Introduction must establish `problem -> gap -> method -> evidence`.
- Related work should be thematic, not a bibliography dump.
- Method must explain design choices and what each component is for.
- Results must foreground the evidence spine, not every experiment ever run.
- Limitations are mandatory.

## Target Sections

1. Abstract
2. Introduction
3. Related Work
4. Method
5. Experiments
6. Results
7. Discussion
8. Limitations
9. Conclusion

## Budget Guidance

- Abstract: `150-220` words
- Introduction: `0.9-1.2` pages
- Related Work: `0.8-1.0` pages
- Method: `2.0-2.4` pages
- Experiments: `1.5-2.0` pages
- Results: `1.2-1.8` pages
- Discussion + Limitations + Conclusion: `0.8-1.2` pages total

## Storyline Guardrails

- Keep at most `3` headline claims.
- Each headline claim must map to evidence in the claim-evidence matrix.
- Figure 1 should communicate the method or the main finding.
- Do not spend main-body space on parked or killed tracks.

## Citation Guardrails

- Every related-work contrast needs a real citation.
- Every key claim in Abstract and Introduction needs either experiment evidence or a citation.
- `refs.bib` must be built from source-of-truth metadata, never invented.
