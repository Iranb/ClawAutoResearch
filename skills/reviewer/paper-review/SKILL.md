# Paper Review

Review the paper through three lenses:

- internal validity
- external validity
- contribution

## Required checks

- claim verification
- overclaim and distortion detection
- support labels for headline claims

Use conservative labels when evidence is weak:

- verified
- minor distortion
- major distortion
- unverifiable

The review should explicitly mention the three lenses and separate evidence-backed criticism from open uncertainty.
