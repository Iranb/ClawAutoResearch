# Journal Writing Mode

Use this built-in mode for journal-style papers with:

- `12` pages main body
- `2` pages references
- deeper methodological explanation and broader discussion
- the same single-paper storyline discipline as conference mode

## Required Narrative Contract

- Thesis still fits in one sentence.
- Journal length is for depth, not for adding extra weak claims.
- Expand motivation, methodology rationale, error analysis, and discussion.
- Keep the story anchored to one coherent evidence spine.

## Target Sections

1. Abstract
2. Introduction
3. Related Work
4. Method
5. Experiments
6. Results
7. Discussion
8. Limitations
9. Conclusion

## Budget Guidance

- Abstract: `180-260` words
- Introduction: `1.2-1.6` pages
- Related Work: `1.0-1.4` pages
- Method: `2.8-3.6` pages
- Experiments: `2.0-2.6` pages
- Results: `1.8-2.4` pages
- Discussion: `1.0-1.4` pages
- Limitations + Conclusion: `0.8-1.1` pages total

## Storyline Guardrails

- Keep at most `4` headline claims, but prefer `3`.
- Extra space should deepen mechanism, boundary conditions, and analysis.
- Do not turn auxiliary experiments into separate narratives.
- Every section should still hand off cleanly to the next.

## Citation Guardrails

- Broader related-work coverage is expected, but only with verified references.
- Use source-of-truth metadata for every bibliography entry.
- Suspicious or hallucinated citations must be removed before submission.
