# Scope and Protocol (Survey)

## Goal

Make the survey boundary auditable.

## Must Include

1. Topic definition and why the scope is bounded this way.
2. Search / screening logic in plain language.
3. Included vs excluded logic.
4. Blind spots, recency limits, or venue omissions.
5. Any fairness warning about what the survey cannot compare reliably.

## Paragraph Pattern

1. Scope paragraph: what the survey covers.
2. Boundary paragraph: what it deliberately excludes.
3. Protocol paragraph: how papers were gathered / screened.
4. Blind-spot paragraph: what may still be missing.
