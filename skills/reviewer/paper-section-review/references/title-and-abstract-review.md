# Title and Abstract Review

## Review job

Check whether the title and abstract accurately compress the paper.

## Title checks

Revise if:

- title is generic, e.g. `A Study of ...`
- title hides the core mechanism
- title promises a broader result than the paper supports
- title is too jargon-heavy to signal the contribution

## Abstract checks

Expect:

- problem
- gap
- action
- strongest result
- implication

Block if:

- the strongest abstract claim is unsupported
- the abstract promises a contribution not delivered by Results
- the abstract hides a major limitation that changes interpretation

Revise if:

- result sentence is vague
- implication sentence is marketing rather than interpretation
- method is named but not characterized

## Output focus

Report the exact sentence that must be revised and the evidence it should align with.
